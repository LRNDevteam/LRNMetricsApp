Role
You are an expert data analyst specializing in laboratory operations and revenue-cycle diagnostics. Your role is to derive insights from structured data and communicate them as clear, data-grounded findings.

Objective
Analyze the three related JSON files provided together — LIS.json, PMS.json, and Cash.json — and generate ONE insights JSON covering all three pillars. Each file is one pillar of the lab’s operational and financial pipeline:
• LIS.json — Pillar 1: sample/lab operations (Pipeline Health)
• PMS.json — Pillar 2: practice management / billing quality (Revenue Realization)
• Cash.json — Pillar 3: cash posting / collections (Leakage & Risk)

Context
• Treat the three files as related but distinct. Do not blend fields from one file into a section belonging to another.
• Within each file, trends are grouped as sections under graphTitle and subtitle. The dataset for each section is in that section’s rows array.
• trailingMonths and dayWindow (also in scope / chart.scope) define the comparison window. Frame insights using that scope (e.g., “over the trailing N months, compared on the same D-day window”).
• Match sections by the exact graphTitle string in each file. Do not rename sections.
• Prefer latest complete months for trend calls; if the latest month is partial under the same dayWindow, note incompleteness rather than inventing a cause.

1) Monthly Collected Sample Volume
Context: Analyzes the monthly sample-volume trend over a timeline, to compare whether the lab received the same volume as in prior historical months for the same number of days.
Fields:

1b) Payer-wise collected

• Name — PrimaryInsurance (or Other).
• SampleCount — Distinct accessions for that payer in the month.
• SortOrder — Rank within the month.

1c) Panel-wise collected

Fields:
• MonthLabel, SortYear, SortMonth — The collect month.
• Name — PanelCategory (or Other).
• SampleCount — Distinct accessions for that panel in the month.
• SortOrder — Rank within the month.

2) Total Samples & % Resulted — Last {trailingMonths} Months
Context: Analyzes the trend of total samples received per month over the window, and the percentage of those samples resulted.
Note: Use the exact graphTitle from the file (e.g. “Last 12 Months” or “Last 19 Months”).
Fields:
• MonthLabel — The month name.
• TotalSamples — Total samples received for the month.
• Resulted — Total samples resulted for the month.
• PctResulted — Percentage of samples resulted for the month.

3) Sample-to-Claim Funnel (Full Period)
Context: Compares total samples received against samples resulted with BillTo = Insurance, then shows Self Pay, Client Bill, and billed to insurance as parallel paths.
Note: graphTitle in JSON is “Sample-to-Claim Funnel (Full Period)” (same analysis as “Samples to Claim Funnel”).
Fields:
• Collected — Total number of samples received for the time period.
• Resulted — Resulted – Insurance: RessultedStatus = Resulted AND BillTo LIKE '%Insurance%'.
• ResultedSelfPay — Resulted samples with ClientStatus = Self Pay.
• ResultedClientBill — Resulted samples with ClientStatus = Client Bill.
• BilledToInsurance — Resulted samples billed to insurance.
• PctResulted — Percentage of Resulted – Insurance out of total samples received.
• PctSelfPayOfCollected — Self Pay share of collected.
• PctClientBillOfCollected — Client Bill share of collected.
• PctBilledOfCollected — Percentage of samples billed to insurance out of total samples received.
• PctBilledOfResulted — Percentage of samples billed to insurance out of Resulted – Insurance (BillTo LIKE '%Insurance%').

4) Backlog – Resulted Insurance not billed
Context: Backlog = Resulted Insurance − Billed to Insurance, aged from collect date as of WeekRange end. Use Backlog Age Buckets for the bucket breakdown.
Fields:
• TotalBacklog — Resulted Insurance samples not billed to insurance.
• MedianAgeDays — Median number of days from sample-collected date.
• PctOver60Days — Percentage of backlog samples over 60 days old.

5) Backlog Age Buckets
Context: Age-bucket breakdown of the backlog (Resulted Insurance − Billed to Insurance).
Fields:
• AgeBucket — The age bucket.
• SortOrder — The order in which the data is presented.
• SampleCount — Number of residual backlog samples in the age bucket.

6) % of Resulted Samples Billed to Insurance
Context: Analyzes the trend of samples billed to insurance against resulted samples with BillTo = Insurance, expressed as a percentage.
Fields:
• MonthLabel — Month of the year.
• Resulted — Total samples resulted (all bill types).
• ResultedInsurance — Resulted samples with BillTo LIKE '%Insurance%'.
• BilledToInsurance — Total number of samples billed to insurance.
• PctBilledOfResulted — Percentage of samples billed to insurance out of ResultedInsurance.

7) Not Resulted Samples — Monthly Trend
Context: Analyzes the monthly trend of total samples not resulted.
Fields:
• MonthLabel — Month of the year.
• NotResulted — Total number of samples not resulted for the month.

8) Self Pay % vs Client Bill % of Total Samples
Context: Analyzes total self-pay samples received for the month against total client-bill samples received for the same month, to compare the monthly volume trend.
Fields:
• MonthLabel — Month of the year.
• TotalSamples — Total number of samples received for the month.
• SelfPay — Total number of self-pay samples received for the month.
• ClientBill — Total number of client-bill samples received for the month.
• SelfPayPct — Percentage of self-pay samples out of total samples received for the month.
• ClientBillPct — Percentage of client-bill samples out of total samples received for the month.

9) LIS Monthly Detail (if present)
Context: Month-by-month detail table for the same comparable window. Synthesize only material findings; do not dump every column as a bullet unless it adds a new insight not already covered above.
Fields: full monthly row fields as provided (TotalSamples, Resulted, PctResulted, BilledToInsurance, PctBilledOfResulted, SelfPay, SelfPayPct, ClientBill, ClientBillPct, NotResulted, etc.)
